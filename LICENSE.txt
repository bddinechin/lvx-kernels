lvx-kernels redistributes work from autovec-benchmark
(https://github.com/sergisiso/autovec-benchmark), which stacks three
BSD-3-Clause notices.  All three are reproduced below verbatim, as each
requires.

Which covers what, in this repository:

  * The first notice (Sergi Siso) covers the kernels taken from
    autovec-benchmark's src/microkernels/.  src/convolution.c is a C
    translation of convolution_serial.cpp and is covered by it.

  * The second (University of Illinois, Polaris Research Group, via the LLVM
    project Test Suite) covers autovec-benchmark's TSVC half, which is NOT
    imported here -- lvx-perf's TSVC suite is lvx-tsvc2, a fork of
    UoB-HPC/TSVC_2 instead.  The notice is retained because it is part of the
    upstream licence file and because the kernels and that half share a
    repository.

  * The third (Intel Corporation, via the ISPC examples) covers the kernels
    derived from ISPC's examples -- ao, mandelbrot, binomial, black-scholes
    and stencil.  None of those is imported yet; the notice is retained
    ahead of them.

The harness, the drivers under lvx/ and this file are first-party and are
BSD-3-Clause under the first notice's terms, to keep the whole tree under one
licence.
